# AI Business Problems and Solutions

| # | Industry | Business Problem | AI Solution | Business Value | AI Category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Banking | Fraudulent transactions slip through manual review | Detect fraud automatically | Reduce fraud and losses | Predictive AI |
| 2 | Insurance | Claims take days to review | Automatically review claim documents | Faster claim processing | Automation AI |
| 3 | Healthcare | Doctors spend hours writing notes | Generate clinical notes | Save doctors' time | Generative AI |
| 4 | Ecommerce | Customers struggle to find products | Recommend relevant products | Improve sales and experience | Customer Service AI |
| 5 | Banking | Support teams get repetitive questions | AI chatbot answers common questions | Reduce support workload | Customer Service AI |
| 6 | Software Engineering | Developers write too much boilerplate code | Generate boilerplate code | Increase productivity | Generative AI |

## The 3 most important measures for a successful AI project are:

1. ***Business Value*** – Does it solve the business problem and improve results?
2. ***Cost/ROI*** – Does the benefit justify the cost of building and running the AI?
3. ***Performance*** – How accurately and reliably does the AI perform?

### Key Takeaway
***Value + ROI + Performance = AI Project Success.***
