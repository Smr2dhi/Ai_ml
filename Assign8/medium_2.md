# AI Industry Use-Case Analysis Matrix

| Industry | Use Case | Business Problem | AI Solution | Business Value | Category | Feature or Product? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Banking | Fraud detection | Fraudulent transactions cause financial losses | Transaction pattern analysis | Reduced fraud losses; detection rate: 92% → 97% | Predictive | Feature |
| Banking | Loan processing | Manual document and application review is slow | Automated document extraction and applicant assessment | Faster loan decisions; processing time: 2 days → 30 minutes | Automation | Feature |
| Insurance | Claims processing | Manual claim-document review takes significant time | Document extraction and summarization | Faster claim processing; review time: 2 days → 15 minutes | Automation | Feature |
| Insurance | Risk assessment | Difficult to identify high-risk policies consistently | Predictive risk scoring using policy and claims data | Better risk selection; accuracy: 75% → 90% | Predictive | Feature |
| Healthcare | Medical summarization | Clinicians spend time reviewing lengthy medical information | AI-generated summaries of medical records | Less review time; summary preparation: 20 minutes → 3 minutes | Generative | Feature |
| Healthcare | Diagnosis assistance | Clinicians must analyze many symptoms and clinical data points | AI-assisted analysis of symptoms and clinical information | Faster decision support; analysis time: 15 minutes → 5 minutes | Predictive | Feature |
| Ecommerce | Product recommendations | Customers may struggle to discover relevant products | Personalized recommendation engine | Higher sales conversion; conversion rate: 4% → 6% | Predictive | Feature |
| Ecommerce | Customer support | High volumes of repetitive questions increase support workload | AI chatbot for common customer queries | Faster responses; response time: 5 minutes → 30 seconds | Customer Service | Feature |

## Why these projects could still fail

1. **Poor problem definition** – The banking loan-processing solution could fail if the real bottleneck is manual approval policy rather than document processing.
2. **Poor data quality** – The banking fraud-detection model could make inaccurate predictions if it is trained on incomplete or incorrectly labeled transaction history.
3. **Lack of domain knowledge** – The insurance risk-assessment system could produce poor recommendations if the team does not understand how underwriting risk is evaluated.
4. **Unrealistic expectations** – The healthcare diagnosis-assistance system could fail if stakeholders expect AI to replace clinical judgment instead of providing decision support.

## Domain Knowledge

Domain knowledge matters because healthcare diagnosis assistance, for example, requires clinical expertise to interpret AI recommendations safely and understand when they should or should not be trusted.
